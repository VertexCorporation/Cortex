## 1. Overview

Cortex ("the App", "we", "us") is an AI companion application developed by
Vertex Corporation. Our guiding principles are **Privacy, Personalization, and
Performance**. This policy explains what data we handle, why, and the choices
you have.

## 2. Two Operating Modes

### 2.1 Offline Mode (Local Processing)
When you use a locally run model, **all prompts, responses, and conversation
content are processed entirely on your device**. This data never leaves your
device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. Conversation history
stored for offline use remains in the app's local storage on your device.

### 2.2 Online Mode (Cloud Processing)
When you use an online model, your prompt is sent through our secure
infrastructure to the selected AI provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, or
Google via OpenRouter). In this mode:
- We act as a secure relay and **do not permanently store the content of your
  conversations**.
- Prompts may be forwarded to our safety/moderation partners for automated
  content review before being relayed to the model provider.
- Usage may be metered against your subscription/credit balance.

## 3. Data We Collect

- **Account data:** email address and authentication tokens (via Firebase
  Authentication) when you sign in.
- **Subscription data:** purchase records and entitlement status (via the
  respective app store and our billing backend).
- **Device data:** non-identifying device information required for model
  downloads, performance, and crash diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics).
- **Crash & diagnostics:** anonymized crash reports and performance metrics to
  improve stability.

## 4. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not sell your personal data. Conversation content processed in Offline
Mode is never collected. We do not use your private conversations to train
models except where you explicitly opt in.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, KVKK, CCPA), you may have the right
to access, rectify, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these
rights, contact **contact@vertexishere.com**.

## 6. Children

Cortex is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent
minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from
children.

## 7. Changes

We will notify users of material changes to this policy through the App or our
website. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
